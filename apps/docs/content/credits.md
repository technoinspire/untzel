## What the app bundles {#bundled}

Untzel plays recordings in fifteen instruments. The recorded sounds of `choir` and the `ooh` and `aah` sounds of `vox` are sung vowels from VocalSet, cut into held notes, tuned to each zone of the keyboard and, for the choir, summed into small ensembles. `piano` plays the Salamander Grand Piano; `strings` and `harp` play VSCO 2 Community Edition; `mallet`, `bell`, `kalimba`, `conga`, `bongo`, `cowbell`, `woodblock`, `shaker`, `clap` and `hat` play the Versilian Community Sample Library. Each recording is downloaded only when a track uses it, and the instrument plays its synthesized voice until it arrives. Every other sound of Untzel is synthesized on your device. The interface uses two fonts, Hanken Grotesk and Jost, under the SIL Open Font License, with the license text bundled next to each.
