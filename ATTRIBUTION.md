## Where and how

The reference implementation shows the attribution in these places. Keeping them
as they are is the simplest way to comply.

| Place | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Top bar of the instrument | the Graphic Image, unmodified, at least 16 px high, linked to the Attribution URL |
| Footer of the instrument | the Attribution Phrase, always on screen |
| Every exported video | the closing card with the Techno Inspire \| Untzel lockup and the Attribution URL |
| Every page that embeds `<untzel-player>` | the player's attribution, visible whenever the player is visible |
| About or legal notice of the interface | the Attribution Copyright Notice and the Attribution Phrase |

The attribution must be legible, must not be hidden, covered, cropped or made
transparent, and must be shown each time the software is launched or initially run.
